# Brand assets

The product includes a CSS-rendered plate, cutlery and receipt mark in frontend/app/main.ts (brandLogo) and frontend/app/styles.css (.brand-logo). The public SVG assets and BrandMark component redraw that same product concept as independent vectors in the website’s charcoal/lime palette. No product imports or dependencies are used. Product screenshots retain the source UI’s original teal/blue mark. These marketing assets require owner brand approval before publication.
